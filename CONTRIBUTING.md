# Contributing

This repository is primarily a public working record for `maitranilim`, but evidence-backed suggestions are welcome.

## A useful contribution

A contribution should include:

1. the learning problem;
2. the human direction or constraint;
3. the observed AI behavior before the change;
4. the revised workflow, rubric, or artifact;
5. evidence that the revision helped;
6. limitations or untested assumptions.

Do not submit private conversations, personal photographs, confidential learner data, copyrighted course material, or claims of improved learning without evidence.

## Adding a new finding

Use the “New learning-system finding” issue template. A finding begins in `contribution-log/`. Promote it into `docs/`, `rubrics/`, `samples/`, or `completed/` only when the pattern is reusable.

## Writing style

- Prefer operational rules over inspirational language.
- Separate direct evidence from retrospective interpretation.
- State what the human did and what the AI did.
- Use “AI-assisted” when model labor was substantial.
- Avoid guarantees about learner outcomes.
