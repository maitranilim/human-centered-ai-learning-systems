## Error-class prompts

- **Concept:** Can the learner explain what is happening and why?
- **Method:** Can the learner choose and execute the correct procedure?
- **Decision:** Can the learner identify which rule or representation applies?
- **Retrieval:** Can the learner reconstruct it without the source open?
- **Transfer:** Can the learner use it in a nearby but unfamiliar problem?

Close an entry only after a blind or delayed retest shows that the same failure is not recurring.
