# Reusable templates

These templates turn the public method into a repeatable workflow.

| Template | Use |
|---|---|
| [Learning session brief](learning-session-brief.md) | Specify the learner problem, mechanism, and acceptance criteria before asking an AI model to draft |
| [Error ledger](error-ledger.md) | Diagnose a failed step and choose the smallest repair |
| [Exit gate](exit-gate.md) | Require evidence before progressing or claiming completion |

Copy a template into your own project, record what changed, and keep outcome claims proportional to the evidence.
