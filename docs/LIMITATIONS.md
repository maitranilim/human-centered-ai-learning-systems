# Limitations

## Evidence boundary

This repository reconstructs the strongest recoverable contribution patterns from available artifacts and interaction history. It is not a complete export of every AI conversation.

## Learning outcomes

The artifacts demonstrate design and production. They do not establish causal improvements in score, rank, retention, motivation, or completion. Those claims would require learner data and a suitable evaluation design.

## Technical accuracy

The educational artifacts were not independently peer reviewed for this release. Exam syllabi, conventions, and official guidance can change. Readers should verify consequential facts against authoritative sources.

## Personalization

The study plan reflects one user’s stated preparation state, time budget, sequence preferences, and target. Reuse the control logic, not the exact calendar.

## Model behavior

The phrase “helped the model improve” means that `maitranilim` supplied feedback that improved outputs and reusable interaction protocols. It does not mean the base model was fine-tuned or its weights permanently changed.

## Authorship

The AI contribution is substantial. The human contribution is strongest in requirements, pedagogy, critique, evaluation, and curation. Both are disclosed.
