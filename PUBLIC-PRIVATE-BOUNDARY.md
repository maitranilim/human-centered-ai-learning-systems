# Public and private release boundary

This repository is the public, usage-ready educator release.

## Public

The public release includes reusable methods, samples, templates, rubrics, case studies, provenance notes, and limitations. Anyone may read, adapt, and test those materials under the licenses in [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md), within the stated rights boundaries.

## Private

The complete production PDFs, DOCX, personalized planning details, and unreleased working material are kept in a separate private repository named `human-centered-ai-learning-systems-private`. They are not licensed for redistribution merely because a reader can see this public repository.

The private archive is active. It is the source for future findings that may later be distilled into a public sample or method after owner review.

This split is deliberate: public readers get something useful to run, while the complete deliverables remain controlled by `maitranilim`.
