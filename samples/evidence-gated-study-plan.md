# Sample: Evidence-gated study plan

A calendar target does not authorize progression. Evidence does.

## Weekly execution

- Keep one active completion subject.
- Pair concept work with examples, PYQs, and closed-book recall.
- End the week with a timed test, analysis, and targeted repair.
- Keep retention checkpoints separate from new-content blocks.

## Exit gate

Advance only when:

- the official scope is mapped;
- available past questions have been attempted and tagged;
- a closed-book concept map exists;
- formulas include conditions and failure cases;
- a timed subject test clears the selected threshold;
- repeated conceptual errors are repaired.

## Anti-rewatch rule

Do not restart a lecture series because confidence feels low. Rewatch only when a diagnostic attempt identifies the exact missing dependency. This replaces mood-based repetition with evidence-based repair.
